Klotski Puzzle Visualization

Goal: Move the large red piece (Big) to the green goal area at the bottom.

Graph: Each node represents a unique puzzle state. The graph shows all calculating... reachable positions.

Interaction: Drag pieces on the board or click any node to teleport to that state.

Distance: 0 moves from start, - moves to nearest solution.

Controls: rotate with A/D, pan with arrow keys, zoom with mouse wheel.

You can also click on any position on the graph to 'teleport' to it.
